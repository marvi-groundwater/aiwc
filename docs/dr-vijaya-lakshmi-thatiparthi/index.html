Vijaya Lakshmi Thatiparthi is a Associate Professor in Environmental Science and Technology and has over 19 years’ teaching, research and outreach experience. Her current research has a particular emphasis on: application of geospatial technologies for water resources studies, mapping land use land cover changes and focussing on SDG 2030. She is coordinating Department of Science and Technology, Govt of India sponsored R&D project and Capacity Building and Strengthening training programs in the field of Geospatial Technology. She is passionate about organizing technical talks, conferences and building network among scientific fraternity. She has more than 30 publications.
