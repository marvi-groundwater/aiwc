Marc Miska is a Senior Lecturer with interest in developing evidence-based decision support tools for industry and government. His focus is the design and development of a data-centric platform for multi-disciplinary data analytics with applications in transport, disaster management, and construction. 

His background in computer-science and civil engineering allows him to integrate research outcomes with existing business and information technology systems to provide an immediate value to clients.

Marc has worked with many industry and government partners over the years and has established long-term collaboration and ongoing funding for his research and has effectively delivered projects in collaborative teams over the years.

Research Project

  1. Estimating waterway Usage and Vessel Speed on Gold Coast Waterways: Phase 2, Gold Coast Waterway Association
  2. Community-focused smart technologies to enhance flood resilience in high risk areas, Logan City Council
