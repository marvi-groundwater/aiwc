Dr. P.K. Singh is working as Professor in Soil and Water Conservation Engineering Department and has over 32 years ‘of teaching, research and extension experience in the field of soil and water conservation, watershed management ,ground water engineering. He has written 9 books, 10 book chapters and published 90 research papers in national and International journals. He received three national awards from Indian Society of Agricultural Engineers and also recipient of University Best Teacher Award in the year 2003 and 2019.He is deeply involved in policy and institutional aspect of water management and also have close liaison with community, NGOs and government agencies working in water sector.
