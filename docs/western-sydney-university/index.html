Western Sydney University is a multi-campus university in the Greater Western region of Sydney. It has over 40,000 students and is in world’s top 300 universities in Times Higher Education ranking. The water research at the University is driven by impact with a focus on sustaining diverse urban, agricultural and natural systems. The University has significant capacity in teaching, research and capacity building in the area of surface and groundwater management, irrigation, stormwater, water quality, river health, cultural behaviour regarding water and water policy and governance.
