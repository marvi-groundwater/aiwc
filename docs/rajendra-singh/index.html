Rajendra Singh has over 30 years of teaching and research experience. His current research focus is on developing a national hydrological model under the National Hydrology Project. He has developed an Irrigation Optimization System (IOS) which combines the hydraulic modelling of canal system and hydrological modelling of the irrigated command to improve the performance of a canal irrigation system. He has further developed an Integrated Reservoir-Based Canal Irrigation Model, IRCIM. He has developed and popularized software packages like Hydrology_Calculator, SCS_Designer and DSS_ET to improve the agricultural engineering education and research in the country. He has more than 100 publications in peer-reviewed, international journals, and he has led several major externally funded research projects.
