Dr. Indranil De is a Professor in Social Sciences and Economics at Institute of Rural Management Anand (IRMA), India. He teaches Collective Actions and Cooperation, Development Theory, Applied Econometrics and Social Cost Benefit Analysis at IRMA. He was associated with several research and consulting assignments with international and national institutes as well as state and central governments of India. This includes Department of Science and Technology (Government of India), Government of West Bengal, Government of Gujarat, Government of Meghalaya, The World Bank, UNICEF, and International Water Management Institute (IWMI). He is the editor of International Journal of Rural Management. He has published several journal articles and books with reputed publishers.

Research Project

  1. Ongoing Research: Innovation Centre for Eco-Prudent Wastewater Solutions (IC-ECOWS) (Co-PI), funded by Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, 2019 – 24
  2. Completed Research: Institution and Socio-economic Assessment of Water Users Association under WBADMIP (PI), funded by The World Bank and the Government of West Bengal, 2019-20
  3. Completed Research: Impact of SSNNL Canal introduction in the arid, semi arid and drought prone area on socio economic environment (Co-PI), funded by Sardar Sarovar Narmada Nigam Ltd., 2018-21
